# StudyMate AI – The Ultimate AI Academic Assistant for BS English Students

## 📚 Overview
**StudyMate AI** is an all-in-one academic assistant specially designed for BS English Language & Linguistics students. It helps students complete academic tasks quickly, accurately, and professionally through an elegant AI-powered interface.

---

## ✨ Core Features

* **📚 AI Quiz Solver:** Solve MCQs instantly, explain correct answers, ensure high accuracy, and practice topic-wise quizzes.
* **📝 AI Assignment Writer:** Generate comprehensive academic content with automatic headings, professional formatting, and clean layout design.
* **🎓 Thesis Assistant:** Get latest thesis topic suggestions, topic refinement, proposal writing support, literature review headings, research gap identification, and APA citation guidance.
* **📖 English Language & Linguistics Expert:** Complete knowledge coverage for Phonetics, Phonology, Morphology, Syntax, Semantics, Pragmatics, Sociolinguistics, Psycholinguistics, Discourse Analysis, Stylistics, ESP, and Literature.
* **📄 Notes Summarizer:** Upload or input text to generate short notes, key points, important definitions, and exam preparation materials.
* **🎯 Latest Thesis Topic Generator:** Discover original and recent research topics in Linguistics, Literature, Applied Linguistics, ELT, Translation Studies, and Corpus Linguistics.
* **📊 CGPA Calculator:** Calculate semester-wise GPA, overall CGPA, percentage conversion, and required GPA predictions.
* **🤖 AI Chat Assistant:** 24/7 academic support tailored specifically for English literature and linguistics students.
